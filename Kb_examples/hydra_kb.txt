## 2. Common Services
**Refrigerators & Freezers:** not cooling, leaking, ice maker issues, defrost or noise problems.
**Washers & Dryers:** not spinning, not draining, not heating, clothes stay wet, making noise.
**Dishwashers:** not washing, leaking, not draining, smells, leaving residue.
**Ovens & Ranges:** not heating, gas ignition problems, uneven temperature, error codes.
**Microwaves:** not heating, sparks, door latch or keypad problems.
**Garbage Disposals / Compactors:** jammed, humming, no power.
**Commercial Appliances:** coolers, ovens, or dishwashers for restaurants or offices.
