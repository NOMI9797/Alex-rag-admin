## 2. Common Services
**Refrigerators & Freezers:** not cooling, leaking, ice maker issues, defrost or noise problems.
**Washers & Dryers:** not spinning, not draining, not heating, clothes stay wet, making noise.
**Dishwashers:** not washing, leaking, not draining, smells, leaving residue.
**Ovens & Ranges:** not heating, gas ignition problems, uneven temperature, error codes.
**Microwaves:** not heating, sparks, door latch or keypad problems.
**Garbage Disposals / Compactors:** jammed, humming, no power.
**Commercial Appliances:** coolers, ovens, or dishwashers for restaurants or offices.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions
**Q1:** Do you charge the diagnostic fee even if I don’t do the repair?
**A1:** Yes, the $120 ($150 commercial) diagnostic covers technician travel and inspection. It’s applied toward the repair if approved.
**Q2:** Can I get a quote over the phone?
**A2:** The technician must inspect the appliance first to give an accurate estimate.
**Q3:** Do you service all brands?
**A3:** Yes, we repair most major **American, Korean, and European brands.**
**Q4:** How long do repairs take?
**A4:** Most are completed on the first visit; parts orders usually take **2–5 business days.**
**Q5:** Do you offer a warranty?
**A5:** Yes — **90 days on labor and 3 months on parts.**
